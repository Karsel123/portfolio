About

Grounded in engineering, sharpened by delivery

I'm a software developer with a foundation in programming languages, machine learning, and Agile delivery. Over two years at Developer Tools Private Limited, I worked across the full lifecycle of software products - from requirements gathering through architecture, development, and deployment - while running SCRUM ceremonies as the team's SCRUM master.
